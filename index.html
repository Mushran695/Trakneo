Features

Trakneo enables organizations to monitor the number of times an asset—such as an advertisement, landing page, or embedded widget—is displayed to users across digital environments.

  • Impressions

  • Clicks

  • Conversions

Impressions

Measure Reach & Awareness Impressions represent how many times your content, ad, or link has been displayed to users. With Trakneo, every view is counted with pinpoint accuracy—whether on websites, social platforms, or ad networks.

  • Track how many users are being exposed to your campaigns.
  • Monitor trends in visibility across devices and geographies.
  • Understand brand exposure and content performance at scale Use impression data to gauge interest, refine targeting, and optimize for higher visibility.

Clicks

Clicks are the first sign of interest—Trakneo helps you understand where, when, and why users are engaging.

  • Get real-time click analytics across all campaigns
  • Identify which ads, links, or calls-to-action are most effective
  • Drill down into device types, referral sources, and geographic data Leverage click data to test creatives
  • improve user experience, and increase interaction rates

Conversions

  • Customize conversion goals based on your business objectives
  • Attribute conversions across multiple touchpoints in the user journey
  • Analyze conversion rates by traffic source, device, or campaign Turn data into decisions. Know which strategies generate ROI and scale them with confidence.

Use On Any Device

Access Trakneo anytime, anywhere—whether you're on a desktop, tablet, or smartphone. Our platform is fully responsive and optimized for all screen sizes, so you can monitor your campaigns, view reports, and make decisions on the go without missing a beat.

Trakneo uses Feather Icons to enhance visual clarity and user experience across the platform. Feather Icons are sleek, lightweight, and built using SVG, making them perfect for modern web and mobile applications.

Retina Ready

Whether you're using a MacBook, iPhone, iPad, or any modern device, your tracking data will always be displayed with stunning visual clarity—no blurriness, no pixelation.

W3c Valid Code

Trakneo is built with W3C-compliant HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, ensuring clean, reliable, and accessible code that follows global web standards. Valid code improves cross-browser compatibility, boosts SEO performance, enhances accessibility, and ensures faster load times—giving your users a seamless experience on every device and platform.

Fully Responsive

Trakneo is designed to be fully responsive, adapting seamlessly to all screen sizes—from desktops and laptops to tablets and smartphones. Whether you're monitoring analytics in the office or checking campaign performance on the go, Trakneo delivers a consistent and user-friendly experience across every device.

Browser Compatibility

Trakneo is built to work flawlessly across all major web browsers, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera. Whether your users are accessing the platform from desktop or mobile, they’ll experience consistent functionality, performance, and design.

Privacy-Focused Data Collection

Have a question? Check out the FAQ

Here are some FAQ questions and answers tailored for Trakneo, focusing on tracking, analytics, and platform usage

What is Trakneo?

Trakneo is a performance tracking platform that helps businesses monitor and analyze impressions, clicks, and conversions across websites, apps, and campaigns—all in real time.

How does Trakneo track conversions?

Trakneo uses tracking pixels, UTM parameters, and custom event triggers to accurately capture user actions like form submissions, purchases, downloads, and more.

Can I track multiple campaigns at once?

Yes! Trakneo supports multi-campaign tracking across various channels, allowing you to compare performance and optimize results all from one dashboard.

Is my data secure on Trakneo?

Absolutely. Trakneo follows industry-best security practices and is fully compliant with GDPR and other global privacy standards.

Do I need coding skills to use Trakneo?

No coding experience is needed! Trakneo offers an intuitive interface and simple integrations. For advanced use cases, we also provide custom event tracking with developer support.

Can I integrate Trakneo with other tools?

Yes. Trakneo integrates with popular platforms like Google Ads, Facebook Ads, CRMs, and web analytics tools to centralize your data in one place.

Ready to take control of your campaign performance?

Trakneo gives you the real-time data, smart insights, and tracking tools you need to optimize every click, impression, and conversion.
